# PlatePay — One plate, two histories

## Director's treatment
- **Promise:** PlatePay connects a merchant's plate read to a driver-approved request, then keeps the completed payment visible to both sides.
- **Identity:** PlatePay's existing deep maritime teal and clear green accent, lifted with warm mineral cream; Space Grotesk display and DM Sans supporting copy.
- **Hero:** A single Kuwaiti plate, treated as the physical thread between merchant upload, driver approval and two transaction records.
- **Motion:** Plate-shaped match cuts; a camera aperture / scan trace; a split-screen that collapses into a shared receipt.
- **Arc:** A sharp plate-led hook, hands-on merchant action, precise recognition, a deliberate driver decision, then a calm two-sided payoff.
- **Payoff:** Two history entries snap into one aligned record beneath the PlatePay wordmark; the closing plate silhouette returns to the opening plate position for a seamless loop.
- **Format:** 16:9 landscape.

## Shot-by-shot script

### Shot 01 / 00.00–04.50 / 4.50s — “ONE PLATE.”
**Purpose:** Establish the exact connective idea immediately: the plate identifies the vehicle, but the people remain in control.

**Composition and artistic idea:** Full-bleed deep teal (#092832), with a giant ivory plate slab occupying the right 58% and clipped by the frame edge. Its two number groups read **7 · 8942** in a Kuwaiti plate layout. On the left, two small role labels—MERCHANT / DRIVER—are joined by a mint line that traces toward the plate. Fine registration corners and a restrained grainless radial light give the plate dimensionality. Safe inset 5%.

**Exact content:** At 00.00, small top-left “PLATEPAY · KUWAIT”; at 00.16, “ONE” pops in oversized; at 00.56, “PLATE.” locks beneath it. At 01.35, “Two people. One clear decision.” appears at 3.2vmin. At 02.25, labels “MERCHANT” and “DRIVER” resolve on either side of a traced line. Plate copy “7 · 8942” remains legible through 04.15.

**Timed choreography:** 0.00–0.18: plate outline is already visible at 0.85 scale; secondary locator ticks are on screen to avoid an empty arrival. 0.18–0.42: “ONE” snaps to scale with a stiff spring. 0.62–0.92: “PLATE.” lands one half-beat later. 1.05–1.70: a mint trace draws from merchant marker to plate edge; driver marker answers at 1.85. 2.30–3.50: plate rotates just 1.5° and eases forward while copy settles. 3.65–4.50: camera pushes into the upper-left plate corner; green outline and photo frame emerge behind it for the handoff.

**Transition contract:** The plate's rounded border is the same shape as the upload-photo frame in Shot 02. Push into the border as a scale-through, then reveal the image behind its opening; combine border expansion with a teal-to-cream diagonal panel fold.

**Assets and technique:** CSS plate slab and legible typography, SVG route trace, masked phrase reveal, scene-local declarative Framer Motion. No external logo asset exists; use the product name as plain text rather than fabricating a mark.

## Pre-code variety review
The consecutive shots vary material: graphic plate object → generated photo plus merchant rail → centered driver card with contour path → macro approval stamp → split ledger/cream lockup. Scale and composition change at each boundary. Each phase has a visible action after its first reveal, and each exit carries the plate, ring, or rule as a physical transition object. Core copy stays large and sound-independent; the merchant-review and driver-consent distinction is explicit.
